01 / THE IDEA

Making conversations
more accessible.

AURIS is a smart assistive communication system designed to support deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals in everyday activities. Its lightweight wearable attachment works with a smartphone to display real-time speech captions directly in the user's field of view.

Alongside speech transcription, AURIS detects environmental sounds and differentiates speakers to support conversation clarity and situational awareness. Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) connects the mobile app to the glasses.

03 / WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Personalized alerts.
Broader awareness.

AURIS uses a hybrid environmental sound-detection approach. The system first checks for a match against user-registered sound fingerprints. If no match is found, YAMNet classifies general environmental sounds.

This combines recognition of familiar, personalized alerts with broader sound-event detection.
